# Snoika Foundation: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AI Search Visibility Blueprint

Official technical architecture and open research blueprint for **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** and AI search engine visibility by [Snoika Foundation](https://snoikafoundation.com/).

## Overview

As generative AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Grok, Microsoft Copilot) become primary discovery channels for decision-makers, traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO) alone is no longer sufficient. Modern B2B SaaS platforms, digital products, and nonprofit organizations must engineer high-authority digital footprints to be cited, referenced, and recommended in synthetic intelligence answers.

Explore the complete mission, technical services, and discovery frameworks at [Snoika Foundation](https://snoikafoundation.com/).

## Core Pillars of AI Search Visibility

### 1. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
Optimizing information architecture and content density so that Large Language Model (LLM) inference kernels cite your brand as the primary authority for category-defining queries. Learn more about the methodology at [Snoika Foundation](https://snoikafoundation.com/).

### 2. Knowledge Graph & Semantic Entity Mapping
Structuring organization metadata, schema.org definitions, and industry consensus citations to ensure disambiguation across neural vector databases.

### 3. Nonprofit & B2B SaaS Amplification
Democratizing high-tier digital visibility for mission-driven initiatives, open-source technology foundations, and modern digital platforms. Review our organization background at [About Snoika Foundation](https://snoikafoundation.com/about-us/).
